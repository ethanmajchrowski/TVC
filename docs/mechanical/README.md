# Mechanical Design

Mechanical components were designed in SolidWorks with an emphasis on minimizing mass while maintaining sufficient stiffness and range of motion for the TVC mechanism.

The gimbal consists of three primary components:

* **Outer mount** — [`CAD/Parts/BodyGimbalMount.SLDPRT`](CAD/Parts/BodyGimbalMount.SLDPRT)
* **Inner mount** — [`CAD/Parts/Inner Gimal.SLDPRT`](CAD/Parts/Inner%20Gimal.SLDPRT)
* **Motor mount** — [`CAD/Parts/MotorMount.SLDPRT`](CAD/Parts/MotorMount.SLDPRT)

The components form two perpendicular rotational axes, allowing the motor thrust vector to be redirected in both pitch and yaw.


### Materials

PETG is currently being used for the printed gimbal components.

PLA was used for early prototypes but was replaced because its comparatively brittle behavior made it more susceptible to cracking under mechanical loading. PETG provides greater ductility and is better suited to the current prototype's expected loading and handling.
